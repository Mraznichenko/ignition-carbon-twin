# Carbon Twin for Work Pitch Notes

## One-Liner

**Carbon Twin for Work turns private employee carbon twins into aggregated company insights, reduction campaigns, and ESRS-style reporting support.**

## Problem

Companies face increasing sustainability data pressure from regulation, enterprise customers,
procurement teams, investors, and employees. But behavior-level carbon data is fragmented across:

- employee commuting
- business travel
- meals and catering
- procurement
- home and remote-work energy
- local climate actions

Many companies are not directly in scope of CSRD, but still need credible sustainability evidence
because larger customers, investors, or procurement teams ask for it.

## Solution

Carbon Twin creates private personal carbon twins for employees and aggregates anonymized insights
into a company sustainability dashboard.

The platform helps sustainability, HR, travel, and procurement teams:

- identify top behavior-linked emission drivers
- recommend reduction campaigns
- support supplier sustainability questionnaire responses
- produce internal ESG evidence
- generate ESRS-style climate transition narratives
- connect teams to action partners and residual carbon removal workflows

Carbon Twin starts as a reporting-support and climate action layer, with a clear path toward
stronger evidence review, approval workflows, and formal reporting integrations.

## AI Angle

Carbon Twin uses deterministic local carbon tools as the numeric source of truth.

AI is used for:

- natural-language profile parsing
- personalized coaching
- explanation and summarization
- ESRS-style narrative drafting

If the model fails, the app still works with local fallback logic.

This makes the architecture more AI-conscious: the model is not used for every calculation, and it
does not invent emissions numbers.

## Enterprise Dashboard

The company dashboard shows AlpineTech SA as a demo company:

- 420 total employees
- 186 employees onboarded
- 3,260 t CO2e/year estimated employee-linked footprint
- 410 t CO2e/year potential reduction identified
- average carbon twin score C+
- 4 active campaigns

Top categories:

- Commuting
- Business travel
- Meals and catering
- Procurement
- Remote-work energy

## Recommended Campaigns

The app converts aggregated insights into practical campaigns:

- Train-first business travel
- Seasonal canteen menu
- Repair before replacement
- Second-hand office equipment
- Residual carbon removal

The principle is:

**Reduce first. Address residual emissions second.**

## Reporting-Support Positioning

Use this wording:

- CSRD/ESRS support
- ESRS-style summaries
- reporting-support layer
- supplier questionnaire support
- internal ESG evidence
- climate action planning

Do not claim:

- CSRD-compliant
- audited report
- regulatory guarantee
- full Scope 1/2/3 accounting

## Privacy Positioning

Individual carbon twins remain private to employees.

Company dashboards use aggregated and anonymized insights only.

The goal is to identify reduction opportunities, not to rank or punish individuals.

## Business Model

Possible business model:

- B2B SaaS subscription
- per-employee pricing
- consultant dashboard
- partner lead generation
- ESG reporting-support workflows
- residual carbon removal or offset transaction flow

## Demo Flow

1. Open the home page.
2. Explain that Carbon Twin started as a personal carbon advisor, but companies are the buyer.
3. Click **Explore company dashboard**.
4. Show AlpineTech SA company profile.
5. Show aggregated footprint and top categories.
6. Show CSRD / ESRS Support card.
7. Show recommended reduction campaigns.
8. Click **Generate ESRS-style summary**.
9. Show marketplace / partner flow.
10. Open employee advisor.
11. Ask: `Should I take train or car from Lausanne to Milan?`
12. Explain that private employee coaching feeds aggregated company reduction insights.

## Final Framing

Carbon Twin for Work helps companies move from fragmented climate behavior data to practical
reduction campaigns and reporting-support summaries.

It is not a compliance replacement. It is the engagement and intelligence layer between employees,
operations, ESG teams, consultants, and climate action partners.
